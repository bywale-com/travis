## What this packet is (one breath)

You dest a seat **on a ticket**. They post. Often they leave a **plate**. That post is **on the ticket**, not only in the room tape. In Voice, Travis does **not** barge the body. He **offers**: about that ticket, this seat has something — Hear it? Skip throws the offer away; the post stays. Hear is a gist. The plate is in the Log bubble, in order. Travis can answer “what’s on that ticket?” and not lie that nobody posted.

That is the whole packet. 2, 3, and 4 are that walk.
