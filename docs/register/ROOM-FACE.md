**Product face:** smartphone web (`travis.app`)  
**Scope:** PM · SA · Engineer only. No external agents.  
**Status:** Locked in **PM-PACKET-002** ([`PM-PACKET-002-ROOM.md`](./PM-PACKET-002-ROOM.md)). Founder approved 2026-08-27.  
**Log:** [`PHASE-ONE-LOG.md`](./PHASE-ONE-LOG.md)

**Main goal (unchanged):** speak with the PM — expanded: **you always speak with Travis**; agents message in the log; Travis reads.

---

## Core law (founder — this pass)

### Travis is the only voice

- When you are **talking**, you are talking **with Travis** (facilitator).
- You may **address** PM · SA · Engineer (“Engineer — …”) but **they do not talk back to you**.
- They **post in the message log**; **Travis reads** their posts aloud (Mode A) or you **read the log yourself** (Mode B).

### Two modes — two views

| Mode | Job | Read aloud |
|------|-----|------------|
| **A · Voice** | Talk with Travis only — orb, tiny Travis subtitle, **no log** | Travis reads everything (default) |
| **B · Log** | You switched to **view the message log** | Optional / off — you're reading |

Switch: quiet **View log** (A→B) · **Back to voice** (B→A).

### Shared chrome (both modes)

- **Travis** serif wordmark top-left (founder liked F2 shell).
- **End session** top-right (small — **not** big bottom bar).
- Room pills: `Room · via Eng` · `Eng · live` (or default PM).
