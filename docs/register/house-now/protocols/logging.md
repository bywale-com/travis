# Logging — every seat, always

A seat is disposable. The log is not. If you do not write the trail, the next person of this role starts from nothing.

## Law

- Unless the founder marks a separate entry, talk lands **in your trail file** — append-only, newest at bottom.
- **Do not edit a past stamp.** Corrections are a new stamp. The only line that moves in place is **Current** at the top.
- Cadence floor in session: at least four stamps a day; denser conversation → denser stamps.
- Notice inflection (flag restated, Type A/B, module enter/exit, founder correction, store minted or refused) and stamp. When in doubt, stamp.
- Capture **founder wording**. Excerpts. Transcripts of the lock, not a generated substitute list.
- Do not scatter one-offs across random files.

Thesis files (`PHASE-ONE.md`) are not the running log. Do not dump the day into the thesis.

## PM grain (the detailed one)

Current pointer at the top. Each stamp: when, kind, founder excerpts, what changed, ask if needed. This is how a new PM starts where you stopped. Almost no loss.
