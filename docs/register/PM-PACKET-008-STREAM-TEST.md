## What this packet is (one breath)

Live work is not the Log. The Log is finished work. While someone is working, you open **In this room**, tap the **glowing** seat, and you get a compartment titled **Stream**. The top line is the message that started it. Under that: the work, in the order it actually happened. When they finish, Stream leaves. A **card** sits **above** the finished message on the Log. Tap the card: same contents as Stream. Same for Travis.

That is the whole packet. Everything below is that, step by step.
