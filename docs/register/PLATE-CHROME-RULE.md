## How a plate is made

1. **Screenshot the live desk first.** Product face = `https://travis-psi.vercel.app`, phone-first. Character as planted: **Mission** unless the pocket is Carbon-only. Open the home the pocket lives on — Voice, Log (Talk or Type), or Backlog (All / In motion / Initiatives). Doors (roster, in-flight, ticket) only if that is the pocket. Hub is not the app. Desktop Cursor is not the app. Do not copy Tower chrome.

2. **Lock that chrome.** Wordmark, dest chips, seat marks, Voice / Log, composer Talk|Type, Backlog tabs, type scale, Mission/Carbon tokens — **as they actually are**. Geometry does not move between characters (`src/theme/scale.ts`: Display 32 · Title 22 · Body 16 · Meta 12 · 4pt grid). Do not invent Home / Inbox / Sequences / BUILD / OPERATE. Do not invent a left nav. Do not redraw the columns. Do not put an iPhone bezel on the plate unless the screenshot has one.

3. **Paint the new idea only in the workspace that already exists.** One purpose per plate. The rest of the PNG is furniture. New control sits where the live control would sit.

4. **Compare.** Header, dest strip, tabs, type, and tokens must match the screenshot. If they drifted, the plate is trash. Regenerate. Do not hand it over.

5. **File it.** PNG into [`plates/`](./plates/). In chat: copy to `/opt/cursor/artifacts/assets/` and show with HTML `<img>`.

6. **Write it.** Images keep the design system congruent. The written spec is the product. Five buckets in the packet ([`PLATE-READ.md`](./PLATE-READ.md)). No extra PNG per channel. Teaching caption is never spec.

---

## Why it keeps the look

The generator is not inventing Travis. It is editing one hole on a locked screenshot of the real desk. Same wordmark, same dest chips, same type, same Mission bone / oxblood or Carbon black / orange. New control sits where the live control would sit. That is the design system — the live app (`src/theme/tokens.ts` + `src/components/Room.tsx`), not a mood board.

---

## Specified and clear

Screenshot first. Lock chrome. Paint only the pocket. Trash drift. Writing wins.

## Specified but not clear (name; do not invent)

- A first-time surface that has **no** planted home yet (no screenshot possible). Then the plate is a first desk — say so. Do not pretend it was a photo of psi.
- Segment vs dropdown paint on a two-value control — same two options, no extra plate.
