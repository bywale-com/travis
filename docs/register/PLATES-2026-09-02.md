## The law they are drawn under

Two modes, one geometry. **Mission** = bone page · carbon text · oxblood accent. **Carbon** = black page · bone text · orange accent. Across modes nothing moves but `page`, `type`, `accent`. Type scale Display 32 · Title 22 · Body 16 · Meta 12 on a 4pt grid. `TRAVIS` wordmark in the wide grotesque; body copy in the shared geometric sans. One loud action per surface; everything else quiet text or icon.

## Engineer refinements carried onto the plates

1. **Unbounded seat marks.** K0 shows `PM · SA · ENG · TRV` and then `AE · QA · DX · R2` under an “unbounded” caption, so a fifth agent has an answer instead of an improvisation. V5 carries a six-badge thought strip with a `+2` overflow.
2. **Failure has a drawn state.** K0 adds an error message kind; V5 and V5-C show a `run error` line in the thread.
3. **Queue at depth with a long label.** The chip reads `3 waiting · Auth Eng`, not a hardcoded short code.
4. **Cost-so-far, not a dashboard.** One muted line on V3 and V4. No chart, no history screen.
5. **Leave ≠ End** drawn explicitly on V1 and in the V5 header.
6. **Manual dispatch** appears as a quiet “send next without waiting” on V6, captioned *Travis does this too* — the parity law made visible.
