**Not 011’s look.** That Pass never opened the glass.

## How it needs to work

You open Travis. You are in a room. The Log is the chat.

You are on the right. Travis and the seats are on the left. You can tell who said what. You type, you send, it is a message in the thread. If you mean someone, it goes to them.

A message can answer another message. You pick that bubble. Reply. You stay in **that** thread — the room Log, or the ticket you are on. The new line hangs on that bubble. It goes **to the person who wrote it**. The quote names **who**. If that line was already a reply, it still goes back to that person. You do not re-address. You do not get kicked to another surface.

If they put a picture in the turn — a plate, a screenshot, anything you can see in the Cursor chat — that picture sits **under that post** in this thread. Not only in Artifacts. Not a dead markdown path. The image is in the chat.

New means a landing you have not seen. Once you have sat on it, or answered, it is not new.

Refresh leaves you in this room, in this thread, at this place.

That is the app that already exists in the logs. It is a chat.
