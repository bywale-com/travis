## Founder wording

> I feel like there needs to be a tighter task pipeline. The requests panel just shows every single thing I’ve texted. I won’t call it requests. Backlog. Travis exists for work management. It should always feel like an initiative.

> It doesn’t have to track every single thing. It just has to track everything that I give to Travis, and Travis passes on. If I give it to the Engineer directly, I’m probably going to be watching that. Personal conversation between me and a seat doesn’t. Anytime I route it through Travis, Travis orchestrates. That pipeline from initiative all the way to done has to be tracked.

> Usually it flows from Travis to PM to SA to Engineer. The PM may not have much to say — they might just be packaging it up. Same with SA. When the PM finishes and responds in chat, it’s responding back to that specific ticket. That’s how Travis knows this person has completed their turn, and whose is next.

> Those messages already look like they’re coming from me. I need a symbol on the chat log itself — a circular icon — that this is a backlog entry. I should be able to hold a message I just sent and turn it into an initiative. That sends it to the backlog and feeds it to Travis to orchestrate. In the ticket I should see something — even canonical chat from each person, and maybe links and artifacts.

## Law

| | |
|---|---|
| **In** | Founder → Travis → a seat. An initiative. One ticket. |
| **Out** | Founder → a seat directly. Personal. You are watching it. |
| **Not Requests** | Every line you typed. Different door. |
| **Pipeline** | Usually Travis → PM → SA → Engineer → done. A seat may pass with almost nothing. That is still a turn. |
| **Reply** | A seat post lands on **that ticket**, not on a pile. Travis reads whose turn completed and who is next. |
| **Mark** | Small hollow oxblood circle on the founder bubble. Not an emoji. Not a seat mark. Scroll the log and you can see which lines are tickets. |
| **Hold** | Long-press a founder line. One loud word: **Initiative**. That writes the ticket and hands it to Travis. |
| **B1** | The index. Stage = which seat marks have lit. Whose turn is the word on the right. |
| **B5** | The ticket. Founding line. One canonical post per seat that has taken a turn. Quiet links and artifacts under that. Next is a muted line. |

## Ticket interior — this read

Not a project page. A thin log of **this** initiative.

1. **Founding line** — the founder bubble, with the hollow mark. That is the ticket.
2. **One canonical post per seat that finished a turn** — the line they sent back into the room. Not their thinking. Not the whole thread.
3. **Quiet attachments under that turn** — a link, an image, whatever landed with the post. Hang them on the seat that made them, not a gallery at the bottom.
4. **Next** — muted word (`Next · SA`). Not a button. Travis already knows.
5. **Open in log** — the door back to the room thread. One door.

What this is not: priority, due date, assignee picker, progress bar, description field, a second comments thread.

Hold is the other door. A founder line that was not already an initiative: long-press → **Initiative**. That writes the ticket and hands it to Travis. A line you already sent via Travis already has the mark.
